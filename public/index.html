The platform

A sovereign workbench for the most personal data you own.

Millions of people have a genome export and almost no trustworthy local tooling. SovereignDNA turns that file into a private, agent-driven workbench — computation stays on your device by default, and collective intelligence is built without ever pooling raw DNA.

Local-first ingest

A Tauri + Rust desktop app with a local SQLite store. Whole genomes stream in at a flat, size-independent peak-RAM — imported once, never uploaded.

Sandboxed skills

Signed skill manifests run inside a wasmi WASM sandbox with no network or filesystem egress — health-risk, pharmacogenomics, traits, carrier status, distributed via a content-addressed registry.

Consent & privacy ledger

Every outbound action is logged and gated by revocable consent. Egress::assert_public_only refuses genotypes by construction — raw DNA can't leave, even by mistake.

Federated intelligence

Opt in to a differentially-private federated network: model updates are DP-noised and secure-masked so only aggregates — never your genome — cross the boundary. Synthetic data today; real-genome FL is legally gated.

Phase 4 · Federated learning

Collective intelligence, without pooling DNA.

Better genomics needs more data — but nobody should have to hand over their genome to get it. SovereignDNA trains shared models with federated learning: your device computes an update locally, differential privacy noises it, secure aggregation masks it, and only the combined, DP-noised aggregate is ever revealed.

The boundary rule

Raw DNA never leaves your device — only DP-noised aggregates cross the boundary. A "clear gradient" update is refused at the device boundary. In the live network, that refusal probe passed.

Differential privacy

A Rényi (ε, δ) accountant bounds each contribution: gradients are L2-clipped and Gaussian noise is added before anything is shared, with a tracked, provable privacy budget.

Secure aggregation

Bonawitz pairwise additive masking means no party — not even the coordinator — sees any single device's update. The masks cancel exactly, leaving only a DP-noised sum.

Live on GKE

A real 3-node round ran on GKE Autopilot on Jul 17, 2026 — aggregator reported ROUND OK with a DP-noised, secure-masked aggregate. Image fl-node:0.1.1, commit 72abe32.

Honest status: the federated network runs on synthetic data only today. Phase-4 production with real genomes remains legally gated — it will not launch without a completed Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA), an independent privacy audit, and epsilon-budget sign-off. We are not claiming real-genome federated training is live.
